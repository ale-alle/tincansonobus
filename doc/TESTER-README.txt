CanDuet test: instructions for testers
======================================

You don't need to install anything or know anything about programming.
Allow about 30 minutes. You and the other tester start at an agreed time.

WHAT YOU NEED
- A Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC (this test version doesn't run on Mac, phone or tablet)
- A webcam and a microphone (built-in is fine)
- Wired headphones. Please don't use speakers (echo) or Bluetooth (delay).
- If you can, plug the PC into your router with a network cable.
- A phone call or text chat with the other tester, in case the app doesn't connect.

START
1. Save the zip file you were sent, right-click it, choose "Extract All", then open the new folder.
   Don't run anything from inside the zip.
2. Close CanDuet if it's already open.
3. Double-click "Join test lesson".
4. Windows may warn you because this test version isn't signed yet:
   click "More info", then "Run anyway". This may appear twice.
   If Windows Firewall asks, click "Allow".
5. CanDuet opens and joins "Demo lesson" by itself. A separate video window opens too.
6. When the video window asks to use your camera, click Allow.
7. In the main window, click "Setup Audio" and choose your microphone and headphones.

You should now hear and see the other tester. If not, see PROBLEMS below.

THE TEST
Talk for at least 10 minutes, and write down:
 1. Your internet provider, and whether you're on Wi-Fi or a network cable.
 2. Sound: is it clear both ways? How many dropouts or glitches in 10 minutes?
 3. Latency and Ping: click the other tester's name area in CanDuet, and write down the
    "Latency (ms)" and "Ping" numbers.
 4. Video: does it stay smooth? Any freezes? Is the picture sharp enough to see hands on an instrument?
 5. Clap test: clap once where the camera can see you. Does the other tester hear it
    before they see it? Roughly how much earlier: none, a little, or a lot?
 6. Close the video window. The sound should keep going.
 7. If you have an instrument, play together for a few minutes. How does it feel?
 8. Optional: click "Tools" at the bottom and try the keyboard, tuner and metronome.

When you finish, close CanDuet and send your notes to the person who invited you.
Include a screenshot of anything that looked wrong.

PROBLEMS
- "Couldn't reach" message, or nothing joins: check your internet, close CanDuet and start again
  from step 3. If that fails, tell the person who invited you. The test server may be off.
- Connected, but can't hear the other tester: check "Setup Audio" on both PCs, and that nothing
  is muted. If it still fails, restart CanDuet on both PCs.
  It may also be a network problem: please report your internet provider and Wi-Fi or cable.
- Video window says the camera is blocked: close other apps using the camera (Zoom, Teams), then
  check Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Camera. Restart CanDuet.
- Video window asks you to install WebView2: follow its link, install it, and restart CanDuet.
- The other tester hears an echo: wear headphones.

PRIVACY
- The sound goes directly between the two PCs, so each PC can see the other's internet address.
  It's encrypted. Only test with people you know.
- The video passes through our test server in Toronto. It's encrypted on the way and isn't recorded.
- Please don't share this folder: it contains the entry pass for this test.
